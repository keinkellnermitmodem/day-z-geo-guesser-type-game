Location workshop

Choose a screenshot, pin the camera position, then download a ready-to-upload location.

N ↑

Use DayZ world X/Z coordinates, with 0,0 at the southwest corner. Altitude is not used. Pin the camera, not the landmark in the distance.

Your image and coordinates will be packaged together.

How do I add it to the game?
  1. Extract the downloaded ZIP.
  2. Upload its image and JSON into locations/chernarus/ in your GitHub repository.
  3. Commit the files. GitHub Actions checks them and publishes the updated game automatically.

You can also skip this editor: name a photo gorka__x9500__z8800.jpg and drop it in the folder. These are example coordinates.
SURVIVOR'S NOTES

Trust your bearings.

  1. Read the scene. Drag to explore the panorama. Roads, hills and buildings are your clues.
  2. Pick your position. Zoom the map and place a pin. You can move it until you confirm.
  3. Get closer. Earn up to 5,000 points per round. Five locations, no repeats within a run.

Imported camera positions are community estimates. Within 30 m earns a perfect score. Standard screenshots are also supported.

Keyboard: focus the scene or map and use arrow keys. + / − zoom. Enter on Confirm location submits.
